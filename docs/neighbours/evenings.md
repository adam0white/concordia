# Two evenings in the reopened hall

The next town episode begins when the hall actually opens. Sam wants to restore the shared supper he promised. Lea wants her displaced practical class back. They need the same room. The host supplies those circumstances; Human Framework chooses what each person does.

There are two opportunities, one and four days after activation. They are not guaranteed events. A candidate is invisible to visitors until its organiser actually posts an invitation. Seeing the reopened hall through HF attention can activate the organiser's existing backstory purpose. That purpose is recorded once, has a deadline and can remain unfulfilled. Proposal and preparation alone cannot complete it across both evenings.

Posting an invitation is an explicit promise to host. Guests must receive the notice before they can accept. Accepting creates a separate targeted HF appointment; ignoring or declining creates none. People can read the outside noticeboard or hear an invitation during an actual shared conversation. A retelling records its speaker and source event. Receiving news creates neither acceptance nor obedience.

Preparation reserves the one usable room for the scheduled hour. Reading and replying at the outside noticeboard uses no room seat. Ordinary incompatible hall activities cannot overlap a prepared reservation. People still have to travel, rest, earn money and choose to attend. No room reservation teleports an organiser or protects their choice from urgent needs.

Attendance records awake overlap during the scheduled hour. A class with no teacher teaches nothing. An empty hosted event gives no social delivery. A missed meal refunds the guest's reserved meal and coin. Shared food is finite and counted in the ordinary conservation ledger. A successful practical class uses the same released HF method-learning path as the earlier timber lesson. A visitor can inspect invitations, promises, actual participation and retained outcomes without a calendar-management screen.

The pilot adds an optional versioned field to the first public save schema. Earlier public saves activate from their current time when appropriate. It never invents past invitations, friendships or missed appointments. New records have strict clock, provenance, room, promise and delivery validation. Ordinary social contact has separate per-job receipts so both HF people develop a relationship once per actual encounter.

## What this tests

The initial unconstrained 208-person seed-42 probe produced no proposals. Adding legal options did not create a lasting purpose. The first purpose-based probe produced a supper attended by three background residents while the class did not happen. These are intermediate findings, not claims about the final deployed rules.

Independent review also caught a host integration fault: people replying to future invitations occupied all the hall's activity slots and blocked the current host. The outside noticeboard now has its own interaction rule. This is a physical host correction, not an HF preference boost.

Controls cover local knowledge, voluntary targeted promises, scarce room booking, interrupted preparation, empty hosting, absent teachers, actual lesson/meal delivery, conservation, truthful failures, retelling provenance and exact restored continuation. The multi-seed profiler records negative outcomes and organiser alternatives as well as successes:

```sh
cd neighbours
node scripts/profile-evenings.ts --seeds 42,73,9,101 --population 208 --days 25 --output /tmp/evenings.json
```

These experiments demonstrate specific causal mechanisms. They do not prove general planning, HF superiority, sustained entertainment or Adam's acceptance of feel. Current measured outcomes and deployment evidence belong in the repository handoff and Beads.

## The workshop bench

After the first evening has closed, Ravi can notice Jo's damaged handcart at the workshop. Its three retained sections take an hour each on the main bench or two hours each at the side bench. Using the main bench requires a received request, Ada's voluntary agreement and a fixed next-day window. Ada's ordinary paid carpentry uses that same bench. Her agreement is a targeted abstention promise; interruptions do not erase the granted window. Delayed earning is an actual opportunity cost, not an invented customer backlog or guaranteed lost wage.

Requests, replies, work, observed help and handover have separate retained evidence. A refusal creates no automatic relationship penalty. Ravi credits help only after actual main-bench use and attention to the evidence; Ada need not be physically present for him to recognize a kept agreement. Completed sections alone do not improve town food. Returning the cart to Jo enables one extra unit on Jo's actual completed supply trips, with the same finite production ledger as other deliveries.

Background names in new towns are stable synthetic identities. They do not imply additional biography or cognition. Existing imported names and histories are preserved.

## Limits of the current project choices

The three cart sections and handover are authored steps. HF chooses among available actions; it has not inferred that repair sequence or the prerequisite relationship between asking and future access. The goal progress amounts are not a calibrated estimate of future value. In the initial four 25-day runs, every cart returned through the slower route and no bench request was made. That is a valid outcome, but it does not prove rational anticipation or autonomous negotiation.

A targeted Astra control found no goal term on asking and identical immediate choice terms for the 60-minute and 120-minute bench offers. The next instrumental-planning probe must first establish an actual received opportunity to ask, then compare access and goal ablations at the same checkpoint. A generic planner requires explicit known consequences and a domain-neutral control. Raising the request score until a preferred seed cooperates would not demonstrate that capability.

The physical bench reservation enforces access. It is separate from HF’s abstention promise. That promise uses `violatedBy: ["work"]`; direct HF controls show work during the window breaks it and no work keeps it. The public host does not currently expose breaking someone else's bench reservation, so its absence is not evidence of voluntary HF restraint.
