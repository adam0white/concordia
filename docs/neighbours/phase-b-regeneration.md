# Phase B: derived cast, HF 2.6.0 and regenerated controls (2026-10-18)

Phase B makes two changes. First, Neighbours behaviour rules in `neighbours/src/core` no longer key on person ids.
Second, Neighbours consumes exact published npm `@adam0white/human-framework` 2.6.0, upgraded from 2.3.0.
Every control listed below was regenerated by rerunning its generator; no digest or hash was edited by hand.

## What changed in the rules

`src/core/cast.ts` derives each town position from the occupation the scenario seeds (`Resident.role`, a host
fact):

| Position | Occupation |
| --- | --- |
| class teacher and organiser | `teacher` |
| supper organiser | `cook` |
| handcart owner | `ferry keeper` |
| cart repairer | `mechanic` |
| main-bench carpenter | `carpenter` |

Answering hall-inspection questions now needs two things: the teaching occupation and retained HF method evidence
for `hall-section-inspection`. That capability covers posting the structural note, answering residents' questions,
reading the noticeboard and receiving instructor suggestions. A class or private lesson transmits the method only
if the actual organiser or teacher retains it. The lesson's evidence source is that person's id.

Rules that used these id checks now use the derived facts:

- `offers.ts`: Lea ×12, Ravi ×6, Ada ×4.
- `runtime.ts`: the suggestion and Lea checks.
- `handcart.ts`, `bench-opportunity.ts`, `evenings.ts`, `stories.ts` and `evening-news.ts`.

Event and label texts are built from the derived residents' names, so the seeded bytes are unchanged.

Id-keyed facts that remain, and why:

- `scenario.ts` seeds who each person is, as the brief allows.
- `state.ts` keeps `known` and `PLAYER_TIES`, which are scenario seed data.
- Import validation still pins each array position to its seeded identity. A renamed save is therefore not
  importable. That is identity validation, not a behaviour rule.
- The save fields `raviGoalId` and `adaGoalId` keep their names so that legacy saves load.
- The player backstory text names Lea and Sam (authored narrative).
- Two presentation lines in `src/ui` name ids.
- Experiment scripts name their subjects.

No hand-set per-person need values were found in `src/core`.

## Is behaviour unchanged?

- **Refactor alone (HF 2.3.0).** All 20 milestone checkpoints across seeds 42, 73, 9 and 101 are byte-identical
  to the 7d2e21e baseline. The four final checkpoint hashes are identical too.
- **HF 2.6.0 upgrade.** All 20 milestones equal the 2.3.0 baseline after normalizing three version stamps: save
  `hf`, person `engine`, and the `HF x.y.z` decision source. Each 2.3.0 milestone restored under 2.6.0 also
  continues for 48 rounds identically to the matching 2.6.0 milestone.
- **HF upgrade notes.** 2.4.0 (promptings), 2.5.0 (`followedBy` bodily forecast) and 2.6.0 (fatigue store) are
  opt-in, and Neighbours opts into none of them.
- **Seeded town.** No behaviour shift was observed: bench choices, attendance, cart outcomes, health and hunger
  are unchanged.

## Regression controls added

`neighbours/tests/cast.test.ts` contains these controls:

- **Seeded cast.** The seeded town reproduces the intended cast. Lea posts the 15-minute structural note and
  teaches. Ada, Musa and Noor get the 120-minute ask and learn instead.
- **Knowledge without the role.** A resident taught by Lea retains the method, but without the teaching
  occupation does not qualify as an instructor.
- **Role without the knowledge.** Lea with her methods removed loses the note.
- **Swapped roles.** The facts move the behaviour: Musa as a method-holding teacher posts the note, teaches and
  receives residents' questions. Elin as mechanic becomes the cart repairer.
- **Renaming (domain-neutral).** Every id in five checkpoints is renamed to an opaque token. The checkpoints are
  reached and arranged at the hall, the workshop and the cart request. Every resident's offers and instructor
  suggestions are equal after renaming. A non-vacuity check confirms the derived episode offers occur.
- **Reordering (domain-neutral).** Reversing the people array leaves positions and offers unchanged.

Mutation checks: reintroducing `id === "lea"` fails three tests, and reintroducing `"ravi"` fails the rename
test.

Whole-run renaming is not a valid control. Seeded per-person streams and priority keys hash ids, and
`seed + i*7919` hashes array order. So a whole run diverges for reasons unrelated to these rules.

## Legacy saves

Continuation is **exact; only the version stamps are migrated**. On restore, the save `hf` becomes `2.6.0` and
each person's `engine` becomes `2.6.0`; every behavioural field is unchanged.

Saves now accepted by restore: HF 2.2.1, 2.3.0 and 2.6.0. `LEGACY_HF_VERSIONS` adds 2.3.0, without which every
save written by the 2.3.0 site would have been rejected.

Saves checked:

- The committed 2.2.1 numbered-neighbour fixture is restored and continued exactly by the existing test.
- A new test restores a 2.3.0-stamped save and continues it exactly.
- Five local 2.2.1 checkpoints restore and continue 48 rounds: the private owner checkpoint, the GM checkpoint,
  the class packet and two owner downloads.
- Two earlier owner downloads lack `firstMealAt`. They were already rejected at 7d2e21e: the field became
  required when the town was first committed (80ebe05). This is not a Phase B regression.

## Negative evidence kept

Every negative finding in HANDOFF still holds on the regenerated controls:

- Natural bench cases choose the side bench.
- The proposal contrast is confounded.
- Sampled underfed peaks occur.
- Some accepted activities deliver nothing.
- No HF superiority or human realism is established.

One HANDOFF claim was wrong at 7d2e21e: it said all twenty stored milestone hashes validate, but the committed
report stored none. The regenerated report corrects this.
