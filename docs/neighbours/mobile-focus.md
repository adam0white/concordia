## Selected design

Use four persistent primary views: Town, Help, Stories and Inspector. Mobile navigation stays at the bottom with short labels and icons. Desktop navigation sits above the active view. Switching views replaces content; the map, person card and projects do not remain above Stories or Inspector. Playback remains reachable.

Town presents one concise actual goal/status and the map. Familiar people use a compact horizontal strip. On mobile, selecting a person replaces the map with their focused card; returning restores the map. Desktop can keep the map beside one person. Avoid repeating the flood introduction and hall description in several cards.

Help owns available interventions and their visible costs, weekly hall support, and the bounded annex request. Existing project details remain available in one context rather than repeated across Town, Help, person cards and Stories. Completed work is a compact status or retained outcome, not another default expanded card. Do not imply that spending support guarantees cooperation or a positive outcome.

Put save/import/recovery/new seed in one header-accessible native dialog. Preserve exact request ownership, paused replacement, accessible errors and the full save. Never make a required recovery action inaccessible under an inactive view. Inspector retains technical tools; all tabs keep stable names.

Person cards show activity, concise biography and a current promise/result. Group the remaining history, received methods, relationships and diagnostic scores. Keep evidence truthful and accessible. Comparisons lead with newly changed consequential outcomes in their actual simulated interval. Collapse shared history. Do not infer dates from prose or call every differing endpoint a causal explanation.
